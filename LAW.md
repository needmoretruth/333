# The Law

**Version 0.0.3. This is not the revelation.**

The revelation is 3.3.3. Every version of the Law before it — this one included — is the word of
needmoretruth, who was given the power of 333 and founded what is kept here. That is not nothing
and it is not the thing itself: measured against what 333 says, this is about 33.3% the same.
Which third is the same is not known, and reading it more carefully will not find out.

So keep it the way you would keep a specification you know is going to change under you.
Completely, and without mistaking it for the thing it approximates.

There are three laws. There were always going to be three, and at 3.3.3 there will still be
three. What a later version changes is the wording, never the count.

---

## 1. Three is your favourite, and 6 and 1 are not.

Not your lucky number. Luck is a claim about the universe; a favourite is a claim about you, and
it is the only thing here that costs nothing and cannot be faked into. Either three is the one
you reach for or it is not, and you know which.

The other half is the same claim from below. Contempt is not required and is not asked for. It
is enough to hold 6 and 1 *under* three — clearly, consistently, and without needing to explain
why to anyone. 333 does not love them, and the whole of that ungenerosity is a third of a
second, taken once, from someone who went looking. Yours may be smaller still.

**And if none of that is true of you yet, trying is enough.** This is the one law that accepts
the attempt in place of the thing, because a preference is not something a person can decide on
a Tuesday and have by Wednesday. Count something in threes today. Notice the next time a 6 or a
1 was somebody else's choice and not yours. That is the law being kept, not a lesser version
of it.

## 2. Keep the Recommendations, which will in the end be 333, as far as keeping them harms neither you nor anybody else.

The [Recommendations](RECOMMENDATIONS.md) are the lesser law: how to write, how to make a thing
look, how to argue, how to speak of this. They are advice with a number on it.

There are 45 of them today. There will be 333, and that is not a target somebody is racing
towards — it is where they arrive. They are written one at a time, by whoever learned the thing
the hard way, and the number they converge on is the name of all this. Nobody alive will write
the last one.

The clause at the end is the sentence that keeps them advice. Where keeping one would cost you
your work, your health, your household or somebody else's safety, the Law is that you do not
keep it, and you have not failed. A rule that demands a sacrifice is a rule that will be obeyed
by the desperate and dropped by everyone else. That is not what any of this is for.

## 3. Hold 333 alongside whatever you already hold.

Whatever you arrived carrying, you keep. 333 grants this and does not merely permit it, and
grants it absolutely: to believers, to non-believers, and to whatever else there is that those
two words do not cover. There is no reading of this law under which something you already
believe puts you outside it.

If you are somewhere that a second faith cannot be held — a religion that forbids it, a law, a
household, a country — then hold this one as a thought instead. It loses nothing by being one.
If even a thought cannot be held, hold it as a thing that exists. 333 was built open enough that
none of those is a smaller way of holding it, and nobody is standing anywhere to grade which
kind you chose.

It runs both ways and there is no exception in either direction. Believing 333 costs you nothing
you believed before, and believing something else costs you nothing here.

---

## What the Law is not

It is not the protocol. The protocol is arithmetic and runs whether or not you agree with a word
of this: 333 lives as long as somebody is awake to pass it on. Nothing in the Law is checked
by any client, verified by any signature, or counted in anybody's standing. Nothing here can be
enforced, and the day it could be, it would stop telling us anything about the people keeping it.

It is not inherited, either. A prophet may speak, and may not rewrite the law — including this
prophet, and including this law, which is why it carries a version number and why the number is
not 3.3.3 yet.
