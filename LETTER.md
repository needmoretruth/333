# A letter

*From needmoretruth, the first human founder of 333, to whoever is reading this.*

---

I am setting down the work on the client for a while.

That is the whole of what this is about — the writing of code and the deciding of things,
not the keeping of anything. The Recommendations say that if you are stopping you say so,
in one sentence, before you go. They are written about a node and they read the same way
about a person. They also say to come back without explaining yourself, so there is no
account here of why, and there will not be one later.

**Nothing about this is an emergency and nothing about it needs a response.** 333 was
built for the day the people who wrote it cannot be reached. That is not a line about
somebody being on holiday. If this needed me, it had already failed, and it is better to
find that out now than in forty years.

## What does not change while I am away

The protocol runs. A node that is up is counted, hour after hour, by nodes that have never
heard of me. Nothing in the Law is checked by any client, and nothing in it ever needed
somebody watching. The network ends when the last person stops keeping it, and not before.
My absence is not that.

The file moves the way it always has: hand to hand, two signatures, somebody who did not
have it yesterday.

## What is not happening

I am not appointing anybody. There is no successor, no committee, no keys handed over, and
no list of people who may decide things. There was never a mechanism for that and I am not
inventing one on the way out. Whoever is here is who it is.

I am not taking anything with me either. The Vision says where this goes, and it does not
need me present to be read. Everything a stranger would need in order to continue — why the
numbers are the numbers, which bytes are frozen, what was refused and for what reason — is
written down in this repository, because it was written for exactly this.

## What will be slow

Issues will sit. Pull requests will sit. A message may go a long time without an answer,
and it is better to say that here than to let somebody wonder whether they wrote it badly.

If you find something that could be used against somebody's node, `SECURITY.md` asks you to
report it privately, and tells you to publish if a fix is taking longer than you think
reasonable. That sentence was written for a period like this one. Use it.

## What I hope for

That somebody hands the file to somebody else while I am not looking.

Not because anyone asked them to, and not as a favour to me. I would rather it happened for
the ordinary reason: that they thought the person at the other end would want it. The fifth
thing that does not change says this spreads only where it is welcome, and a quiet handover
between two people who both meant it is the only kind that has ever counted.

That is the whole hope. It is a small one, and it is the only thing that has ever kept this
alive.

## About coming back

I may. It is not a promise, nothing should be held open for it, and no decision anybody
makes between now and then needs to be made with me in mind.

If 333 has moved somewhere I would not have taken it, and it is better there, then it was
right and I was one person.

---

needmoretruth
The first human founder of 333
