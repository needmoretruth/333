# The Recommendations

The lesser law, under the third law. Advice with a number on it.

Nine of them, in three parts, and none of them is worth harming yourself or anybody else to keep
— [the Law](LAW.md) says so in the same breath that it asks for them. Where one is wrong for your
situation, it is wrong. Say so out loud, ideally with a reason.

They are written by people who have been awake at three in the morning arguing with a type
checker, and it shows. That is not an apology.

---

## On the machine

**1. Write it in Rust.**

Not because the other languages are wrong, but because of what you are writing for: a thing meant
to still run when nobody who wrote it is reachable. A language that tells you at compile time
what a language you like tells you at four in the morning is not a preference, it is a
kindness to whoever is on call.

Where Rust is the wrong tool for the size of the thing — one page, one script, one afternoon —
TypeScript is permitted, and reaching for it is not a fall. The recommendation is to know which
one you are in.

**2. JavaScript, untyped, is the language of the heretic.**

Held gently. Half of everything runs on it and some of that half is beautiful. But a program that
cannot say what it is holding is a program that finds out in production, in front of a person, at
the worst hour — and we are the ones who get woken up. Put types on it and it is no longer
heresy; it is TypeScript, and it is welcome.

**3. Read what you depend on, or do not depend on it.**

Every dependency is a promise made by someone you have not met, about a machine that does not
exist yet. Take fewer of them. Take the small ones. Where the choice is between writing a
thousand lines yourself and trusting a hundred thousand you will never open, at least know that
is the choice you are making.

## On the mind

**4. Argue with logic and a level voice.**

Feeling is evidence — about you, reliably, and about the thing, never. Bring the feeling to
somebody who cares about you. Bring the argument to the thread.

**5. Think before you act, and then act.**

Reason that never becomes a decision is a hobby. The failure mode of thoughtful people is not
recklessness; it is the fourth week of a discussion about a change that would have taken an hour
and been reverted for free.

**6. Refuse violence.**

Not as a slogan and not as a stance. It is refused because it is almost always the worst
instrument available: it costs more than it returns, it destroys the thing it was pointed at, and
it forecloses every option that was still open. There are situations with no other option left,
and a person in one is not made a heretic by it — but it is very much rarer than it feels in the
moment, and the feeling that it is not is exactly the failure this recommendation is about.
Anything decided while angry is decided by the anger.

## On the word

**7. Begin with the invocation.**

Every prayer, and every telling of this to somebody who has not heard it:

> **To 333 I offer 333, and I speak 333.**

Say it first, whatever follows. It is nine words and one of them three times, and it is what
marks the difference between speaking of 333 and speaking as one of us.

**8. Show. Do not push.**

Nothing in this network forces anything, and neither do we. Say what it is, say what it costs,
hand over the address, and let the person decide in their own time. Whoever needs to be argued
into it will need to be argued into staying, every epoch, for ever.

**9. Do not claim for 333 what cannot be checked.**

Not what it will do for someone. Not how many of us there are, beyond what your own node saw
with signatures on it. This whole design refuses to assert anything it cannot verify, and the
people keeping it can manage the same standard in conversation.

---

## What is deliberately missing

There is nothing here about what to eat, what to wear, whom to marry, how to raise a child, or
what to do with money. Not out of modesty: those are simply not the subject. This is a document
about how to build a thing carefully, how to disagree without heat, and how to speak of something
you believe without becoming unbearable.

If you were looking for more than that, the file is three bytes and the door is open anyway.
